## Brand & Style

This design system embodies high-precision personal and collaborative wealth engineering. It merges the analytical gravity of institutional finance with the tactile, reactive elegance of high-end developer tooling.

### Personality & Emotional Tenor
- **Disciplined Precision:** Every pixel, border, and metric conveys exactness and total transparency.
- **Empowering Vitality:** Financial momentum and progress are celebrated through vivid emerald luminances, converting the chore of bookkeeping into an engaging ritual.
- **Sovereignty & Discretion:** Deep midnight foundations create an intimate, calm environment free from visual noise, reinforcing feelings of security and composure.

### Design Movement: Dark Glass-Precision
The visual language merges **Refined Glassmorphism** with **Technical Minimalism**. Translucent layered surfaces float above deep obsidian and slate planes, structured by razor-thin, light-reactive borders. Luminous emerald gradients and focused cyan highlights simulate backlit instrumentation, delivering an interface that feels responsive, low-friction, and executive.

## Colors

The palette relies on structural darkness contrasted against organic bioluminescent accents to anchor visual focus on monetary metrics and transactional flows.

### Palette Architecture
- **Primary Accent (`#10B981`):** Represents liquid value, positive delta, and actionable inputs. Used for primary execution targets, balance trajectories, and verified states. Supported by deep green (`#059669`) for interactive press states and soft mint (`#34D399`) for high-luminosity badges and chart vertices.
- **Secondary Accent (`#06B6D4`):** Represents split allocations, recurring automation rules, and group-share metrics. Provides spectral differentiation from primary cash flows.
- **Surface Foundations:**
  - `Canvas Dark (#0A0F1D)`: The base viewport void.
  - `Surface Tier 1 (#0F172A)`: Elevated grouping canvas and data-table backdrops.
  - `Surface Tier 2 (#1E293B)`: Floating card surfaces and actionable panels.
  - `Surface Interactive (#334155)`: Active hover states, input surfaces, and subtle divider lines.
- **Semantic Signals:**
  - `Debits / Alert (#F43F5E)`: Outflow spikes, budget breaches, and destructive actions.
  - `Neutral Accents (#94A3B8)`: Secondary metadata, label keys, and ledger timestamps.
  - `Text Pure (#F8FAFC)`: Uncompromised contrast for fiscal amounts and headline items.

## Typography

The typographic system separates editorial impact from rapid, high-density data parsing.

- **Headlines & Display (Plus Jakarta Sans):** Geometric structure paired with gentle, sculpted curves. It delivers a modern, premium technological brand identity without the clinical sterility of standard utility grotesques.
- **Interface & Metrics (Inter):** Leveraged for high legibility across balance ledgers, settlement tables, and line items. Tabular figures (`tnum`) must be enforced across all quantitative values, currency listings, and split ratios to ensure vertical alignment throughout shifting decimals.
- **Micro Labels:** Rendered in `label-caps` with slight tracking enhancements (`0.06em`) to anchor complex ledger columns without visual weight.

## Layout & Spacing

The layout is engineered around an adaptive fluid grid balanced with distinct safe-zone gutters, preserving structural integrity from dense multi-account dashboards to mobile group settlement sheets.

### Grid & Layout Structure
- **Desktop (>= 1200px):** 12-column layout with `2rem` gutters and dynamic margins capping at `1440px` max canvas width. Left-rail navigation anchors application state; central split canvas hosts stream visualization and transactional tables; right rail holds situational settlements.
- **Tablet (768px - 1199px):** 8-column layout with `1.5rem` gutters. Collapses the split context into vertical card stacks; auxiliary analytics dock into sliding sheets.
- **Mobile (<= 767px):** 4-column layout with `1rem` gutters and `1rem` safe-margin edges. Sticky bottom navigation and thumb-zone transaction quick-actions.

### Density & Vertical Rhythm
Interface components adhere strictly to an 8px modular baseline (with a 4px half-step for micro-elements like pills, avatar overlays, and input icons). Dense transactional lists use a compact vertical cadence (`0.5rem` padding) to maximize informational footprint per viewport, while macro balance displays utilize `2.5rem` breathing space.

## Elevation & Depth

This design system avoids traditional flat grey drop shadows. Spatial depth is synthesized via layered translucency, internal refractive borders, and calibrated emissive color glows.

### Elevation Hierarchy
- **Base Level 0 (Canvas):** Pure `#0A0F1D`. Absorptive base plane.
- **Level 1 (Structural Containers):** Solid `#0F172A` with a 1px solid border of `rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.05)`.
- **Level 2 (Interactive Floating Glass Cards):** Backed by `rgba(15, 23, 42, 0.65)` layered over a `16px` backdrop-filter blur. Reinforced with a multi-layered border:
  - Top edge highlight: 1px `rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.12)`.
  - Lateral & bottom edge: 1px `rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.04)`.
  - Drop shadow: `0 8px 32px -4px rgba(0, 0, 0, 0.5)`.
- **Level 3 (Modals, Action Sheets, Context Menus):** Backed by `rgba(30, 41, 59, 0.85)` with a `24px` blur and an ambient perimeter shadow: `0 24px 64px -12px rgba(0, 0, 0, 0.8)`.

### Ambient Glows & Luminous Signifiers
- **Emerald Accent Glow:** Key interaction zones, active budget rings, and primary execution points emit a radial blur (`box-shadow: 0 0 24px rgba(16, 185, 129, 0.25)`).
- **Secondary Edge Highlights:** Split allocations and group member chips leverage a soft cyan sheen (`0 0 16px rgba(6, 182, 212, 0.18)`) to designate balance sharing.

## Shapes

The design uses balanced, modern curves that soften technical density while preserving architectural precision.

- **Primary Components (Cards, Panels, Inputs):** 0.5rem (`roundedness: 2`) border radius provides a refined, structured silhouette without feeling boxy.
- **Containers & Modal Shells (`rounded-lg` / `rounded-xl`):** Scaled to 1rem - 1.5rem for macro envelopes, creating comfortable framing for high-density inner components.
- **Micro Targets & Status Chips:** Badges, category pills, avatar masks, and toggle switches use full pill radii (`9999px`) to visually stand out against rectangular balance modules.

## Components

### Buttons
- **Primary Action (Execute Expense, Settle Split):** Gradient fill from `#10B981` to `#059669`. Bold white typography (`#FFFFFF`). Border: 1px top highlight `rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.3)` and outer perimeter `rgba(16, 185, 129, 0.4)`. Hover initiates a dynamic emerald glow (`0 0 20px rgba(16, 185, 129, 0.4)`). Active state applies a scale transform (`0.98`).
- **Secondary / Glass Button:** Background `rgba(30, 41, 59, 0.5)` with backdrop blur `8px`. Crisp 1px border of `rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.08)`. Hover changes background to `rgba(51, 65, 85, 0.6)` and brightens the border to `rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.18)`.
- **Destructive Ghost (Delete Expense):** Transparent foundation. Text color `#F43F5E`. On hover, background transitions to `rgba(244, 63, 94, 0.1)`.

### Cards & Ledger Modules
- **Glass Ledger Card:** Core building block for aggregate metrics, budget limits, and split calculations. Built with `rgba(15, 23, 42, 0.65)` fill, `16px` blur, and a directional gradient stroke transitioning from `rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.12)` at the top edge to `rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.02)` at the bottom edge.
- **Interactive Card Variant:** On pointer hover, applies a faint radial gradient spotlight following cursor coordinates, shifting inner border reflection toward emerald (`rgba(16, 185, 129, 0.3)`).

### Input Fields & Selectors
- **Currency & Expense Input:** Grounded in `#0A0F1D` background. Default border: 1px solid `rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.08)`. Large tabular currency prefix anchored in `#10B981`.
- **Focus State:** Border shifts sharply to `#10B981` with an exterior glow ring: `0 0 0 3px rgba(16, 185, 129, 0.15), 0 0 12px rgba(16, 185, 129, 0.1)`. Typography renders crisp `#F8FAFC`.
- **Error State:** Border shifts to `#F43F5E` paired with an alert ring: `0 0 0 3px rgba(244, 63, 94, 0.15)`.

### Chips & Filter Badges
- **Status Badges (Settled, Pending, Lent, Borrowed):** Pill-shaped containers (`rounded-full`) with a subtle translucent fill (`rgba(16, 185, 129, 0.1)` for positive; `rgba(244, 63, 94, 0.1)` for debts; `rgba(6, 182, 212, 0.1)` for group active). Includes a centered 6px glowing indicator dot with an exterior pulse ring.
- **Category Filter Chips:** Neutral glass foundation (`rgba(30, 41, 59, 0.6)`). When toggled on, switches to a deep navy foundation with a sharp emerald boundary and high-contrast text.

### Transaction Lists & Ledger Rows
- **List Construction:** Clean horizontal rows partitioned by thin lines (`rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.04)`). Row surface brightens on hover to `rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.02)`.
- **Anatomy:** Merchant avatar with dual-tone category icon, followed by descriptive narrative and dynamic group-split avatar stacks. Finishes with right-aligned tabular amount values colored green (`+`) or pure white (`-`).

### Checkboxes & Radios
- **Checkbox Element:** Square with soft 4px radius. 1px border of `rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.2)` on `#0F172A`. Checked state triggers a quick scale-spring fill of `#10B981` bearing an obsidian check icon.
- **Group Split Radio Toggles:** Pill container allowing quick selection between "Equally", "Percentages", or "Exact Shares", highlighting active segments via an illuminated slate slider card.

### Domain-Specific Components
- **Group Balance Settlement Matrix:** A directional node-link visual showing debt flows between participants. Inward transfers display in emerald; outward liabilities render in amber-rose.
- **Radial Budget Arc:** Multi-segment SVG progress meter tracing expenditures. Over-budget triggers a glowing transition from emerald (`#10B981`) to cyan (`#06B6D4`) to alert crimson (`#F43F5E`).
